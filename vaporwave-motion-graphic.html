Project Overview

This motion graphic is probably my number one favorite. For one, I've always loved the theme/aesthetic of synthwave or vaporwave. It just feels like home. However, tracing out a car was more challenging than I thought, and creating the texture on it so it actually felt like a vehicle and not a super flat 2D object was a toughy. We got through it though! In the original storyboard, the sun was supposed to gradually come down not from the top, and be more subtle in the background with a horizon over an ocean in the distance. I did not want to play with reflection settings at the time I made with this, so I had to omit that part to do what I was capable of, and unfortunately disregard the ocean part which would've made the graphic feel like it was actually moving, and not a car gliding across a flat surface. The lights were probably one of my fun little tidbits next to the gleam of the sun as it passes the car, almost like the little reflection you'd get from the suns last burst of brightness before it completely sets.
