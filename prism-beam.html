Project Overview

This one was easy and difficult all at once. Kind of like a Macaron. This graphic is exactly just like that. It had simple assets to create, and it all just felt very basic, like it wasn't supposed to be difficult. Well I was wrong. It wasn't hard per se, but Masks and Layer Blends are still tricky for me, which I had to play around with a lot in this one. The mask thankfully was a little easier to manipulate, as you can see with the arc towards the end as the light beams were restrained within a circle you can't see. (MAGIC TADA!) However, if it weren't for the special effects, it wouldn't come out as good as it did with the refractions gradually glowing towards the end. Do I think I could've done a lot better with it and made it smoother? Absolutely. Do I regret not doing more than I should? Not really. It would've been nice, but I'll be honest, I did this combined with the Vinyl in one day and I didn't want to test the waters of doing more than necessary with the limited amount of time I had.
