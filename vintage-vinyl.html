Project Overview

My Vintage Vinyl Motion Graphic felt like one of those that I overthinked too hard with. I never really thought about drawing a record player, and I didn't know how to make sure it looked proper. So creating the asset for it was surprisingly smoother than I anticipated it would be, but I still had that initial intimidation when I began working on it. The graphic in the animation wasn't as strong as I intended to, and wasn't easy to manipulate in the way I wanted to. If I could, I would have the warp effect that sucks the record player in be a bit more dramatic, and the wave form that appears after to be an actual audio wave that moved, not shook statically. But I made with it what I could, and I think the outcome of at least publishing something that I worked hard on is better than not publishing something I wasn't sure about.
